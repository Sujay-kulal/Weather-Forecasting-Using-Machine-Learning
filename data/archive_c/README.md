# Weather-Driven Indian Power Demand Dataset

> **A curated, analysis-ready dataset integrating Indian state-wise
> electricity demand with weather observations for Data Engineering,
> Machine Learning, Time-Series Forecasting, and Business
> Intelligence.**

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Overview

The **Weather-Driven Indian Power Demand Dataset** is a curated
collection of cleaned and integrated datasets prepared to enable
data-driven research and development in the power and energy domain.

The dataset combines electricity demand statistics with weather
variables through a structured ETL workflow. It has been processed to
improve data quality, consistency, and analytical usability, making it
suitable for predictive modeling, feature engineering, dashboard
development, benchmarking analytics, and academic research.

This dataset is intended for students, researchers, data engineers,
analysts, and machine learning practitioners working on forecasting and
intelligent energy analytics.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Dataset Contents

### 1. PSP_Weather_Merged_EDA_Cleaned.csv

Primary integrated dataset containing state-wise electricity demand with
corresponding weather observations after preprocessing and cleaning.

### 2. Statewise_Monthly_Demand.csv

Monthly electricity demand summaries for Indian states.

### 3. Statewise_Seasonal_Demand.csv

Season-wise electricity demand trends and aggregated statistics.

### 4. Statewise_Full_Correlations.csv

Correlation matrix between electricity demand indicators and weather
variables.

### 5. Statewise_CWI_and_Correlation_Complete.csv

Composite Weather Index (CWI) along with correlation metrics for
comparative analysis.

### 6. Statewise_Hot_vs_Mild_Demand.csv

Comparison of electricity demand under hot and mild weather conditions.

### 7. state_reports_index.csv

Index of generated analytical reports and dataset references.
